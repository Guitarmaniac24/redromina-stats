Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

What we read

With the channel owner's approval, RedRomina Stats reads the RedRomina channel's YouTube stats: views, watch time, subscribers gained and lost, traffic sources, search terms, audience retention and thumbnail impressions and click rate. It also confirms the channel's ID.

What we can't do

The app asks Google only for read-only access. It can't upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

How we use it

The stats are used only to help the channel's team plan and improve its videos. We don't sell them, share them for advertising or use them to train general AI models.

Where it's kept

The stats and access token are stored on a private computer run by the channel's team. Stats may be kept until the channel owner asks us to delete them.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The channel owner can ask the team to delete the saved stats.

RedRomina Stats' use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
